Drop English Aliases that would make an Input ambiguous: those equal to a different Entity's Name, unless a Same-as link joins the two, and those held by more than one Entity. "Norfolk" is no longer an Alias of Norfolk Island, since it is the Name of the Subdivision GB-NFK, and "Congo", "JA", "Saint Martin" and "St Martin" no longer match two Countries each.
